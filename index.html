My Design Philosophy

My design identity is Clean, Simple, and User-First. I don't design to impress, I design to make people understand.

1. Clarity First

Every button, text, and color should have a purpose. If the user needs to think too much, my design failed.

2. Responsive Always

Most users in Candon use phones. So my websites must look good on mobile first, then on desktop.

3. Consistent Identity

I use one palette - dark + yellow, one font, and one style. That consistency is what defines my identity as a developer.
